# Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Excel and data modelling techniques.

## Dashboard Preview

![Retail Sales Dashboard](Dashboard/Retail%20Dashboard%20Image.jpeg)

## Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales transactions to provide insights into revenue, quantity sold, product performance and profitability.
## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyse product and category performance.
- Identify sales trends across months and days.
- Compare sales across different sales types.
- Understand payment mode distribution.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %

## Dashboard Features

- Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Category
- Daily Sales Trend
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Sales by Sale Type
- Interactive Year and Month filters

## Data Model

The report uses two related tables:

- InputData – Sales transaction information.
- MasterData – Product and pricing information.

A one-to-many relationship connects the product master table with the sales transaction table through Product ID.
