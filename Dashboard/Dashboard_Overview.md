# Dashboard Overview

## Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

### 1. Project Overview

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard is developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse retail sales performance, product performance, revenue, costs and profitability.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales transactions and helps users understand business performance across different time periods, product categories, sales types and payment methods.

### 2. Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyse total quantity sold.
- Track total profit and profitability.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Analyse monthly and daily sales trends.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Compare performance across different sales types.

### 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPI metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total sales revenue after discounts |
| Total Quantity | Total quantity of products sold |
| Total Profit | Sales revenue minus product cost |
| Profit Margin % | Profit as a percentage of sales revenue |

### 4. Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters for:

- Year
- Month Name
- Sale Type
- Payment Mode

These filters allow users to analyse performance for selected periods and business categories.

### 5. Dashboard Visualisations

The dashboard contains the following visualisations:

1. Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis
2. Sales by Payment Mode
3. Sales by Product
4. Daily Sales Trend
5. Sales by Sale Type
6. Sales by Product Category

### 6. Data Model

The report uses two related tables:

- **InputData:** Contains transaction-level sales information.
- **MasterData:** Contains product details, categories and pricing information.

A one-to-many relationship connects the product master table with the sales transaction table.

### 7. Technology Stack

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Modelling
- Data Visualisation

### 8. Expected Business Benefits

- Improved visibility into sales performance.
- Better understanding of product profitability.
- Identification of high-performing products and categories.
- Improved monitoring of sales trends.
- Faster and more informed business decisions.

### 9. Project Status

This is a portfolio project demonstrating Power BI reporting, data modelling, DAX calculations and interactive dashboard development.
