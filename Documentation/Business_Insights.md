# Business Insights – Retail Sales Analytics

## 1. Executive Summary

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard provides an overview of sales performance, product contribution, category performance, payment methods and sales trends.

The dashboard helps business users monitor revenue, profitability and sales distribution across different business dimensions.

## 5. Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard provides monthly and daily sales analysis to help identify sales fluctuations and monitor performance over time.

Business users can use these visuals to:

- Compare monthly sales and profit.
- Identify high- and low-performing periods.
- Monitor daily sales fluctuations.
- Evaluate performance using year and month filters.

## 6. Payment Mode and Sales Type

The dashboard provides interactive comparisons across payment modes and sales types.

These analyses help business users understand sales distribution and compare different transaction channels.

## 7. Business Recommendations

1. Review the performance of lower-contributing categories to identify improvement opportunities.
2. Monitor the availability and sales performance of leading products.
3. Analyse monthly sales fluctuations to support inventory and sales planning.
4. Monitor profitability alongside revenue when evaluating business performance.
5. Use payment mode and sales type analysis to support operational decisions.

## 8. Conclusion

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard converts transaction-level data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI visualisations.

It supports sales monitoring, product analysis, category comparison and profitability tracking.
